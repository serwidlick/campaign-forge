# Arcs, sessions and journals

Play is organised in three levels: the **campaign** contains **arcs**, arcs contain **sessions**, and **journal** entries can hang off any of the three.

## Arcs

An arc is a chapter of the campaign: the thing the party is caught up in for a stretch of sessions. It has a status, active or complete, a body for the plan, and a list of its sessions. At the bottom, a **Journal** section lists the entries written about the arc, with a button to write a new one.

## Sessions

A session is one evening at the table. Its panel holds:

- **Arc**: which arc it belongs to.
- **Number**: the session's number in the campaign. New sessions take the next free number.
- **Date**: the real-world day you play.
- **Status**: planned or played.
- **In-world date**: when it happens in your calendar, so it appears on the timeline.
- **Beats**: the ordered plan for the evening. Add beats, reorder them, mark one as the **main encounter** and one as the **cliffhanger**, tick them off as they happen.

The body is for anything else: the recap you read out, notes, loot, what changed.

Beats are prep. They never go to players, even when the session is shared; players get the number, date, status and the body.

## Journals

A journal entry is a dated note. It belongs to the campaign as a whole, to an arc, or to one session; pick which in its **Belongs to** dropdown. It can also carry an in-world date, in which case it appears on the timeline.

Ways to write one:

- **Journal** in the sidebar lists every entry newest first, grouped by month, with filters for campaign, arc and session entries. **Entry** writes a new one for today.
- Every arc and session ends with a Journal section and a **New entry** button that attaches the entry there.

Entries are ordinary elements, so they take @-links, pictures and sharing like anything else. A shared entry is a good way to hand players the recap.

## Showing play on the timeline

Sessions and journal entries with an in-world date, and events, all appear on the [timeline](timeline-and-calendar.md).
